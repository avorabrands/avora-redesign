How long does a project take?+
A logo takes 3–5 days, a full brand identity 1–2 weeks, a website 7–10 days and a mobile app design 10–15 days. Print timelines depend on the product and are confirmed with your proof. You get a written timeline before we start.
How much does it cost?+
Print has fixed prices (visiting cards from ₹3 each, letterheads from ₹19). Design is quoted per project after a free 15-minute call, so you only pay for what you need. Get a quote →
Can I order Diwali hampers in bulk with my logo?+
Yes. We brand the box, card, ribbon and products, and deliver to the address you share with us. See Diwali gifting →
Are revisions included?+
Always. The number of rounds depends on the package and is agreed upfront, so there are no surprises.
Do you work outside Bengaluru?+
Yes. We're based in HSR Layout, Bengaluru with roots in Madanapalle, and we deliver print and gifts across India. Design work happens fully online.
What do you need from me to start?+
A short brief about your business and audience, any references you like, and existing assets (logo, colours, fonts). We send a simple questionnaire to make this easy.
