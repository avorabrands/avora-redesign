Questions

Parents usually ask

Which age is each kit for?

Every kit shows an age badge (3–5, 6–8 or 9–12). Younger kids may need a little help from a parent, which is half the fun.

Can I add my child's name, photo or drawing?

Yes. Storybooks, name puzzles, frames, T-shirts and drawing-to-toy gifts are made with your child's name, photo or artwork. We share a preview before we make it.

How do I order and pay?

Add items to your list and send it on WhatsApp. We reply with prices, photos and delivery dates. Once you confirm and pay, we make and pack your order.

Do you deliver?

Yes, to the address you give us. Personalised items need a few extra days, so tell us your date and we will plan around it.

Can schools or parties order in bulk?

Yes. We make mini kits for return gifts, classrooms and camps, with names on each pack.
