Design FAQ

Before you brief us.

What do I get at the end?+
All final files in every format you need (AI, PDF, PNG, SVG and Figma where relevant), plus guidelines so your team can use them correctly. The files are yours.
How many revisions are included?+
It depends on the package and is written into your proposal. Most clients approve within the first or second round.
Can you also build the website or app?+
We design and hand over developer-ready Figma files. If you need development, we can work with your developers or recommend a trusted partner.
Do you design in Telugu or Hindi?+
Yes — from a 64-page Telugu monthly magazine to Hindi typography in devotional apps.
