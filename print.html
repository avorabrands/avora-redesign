Print FAQ

Print, explained.

New to printing? Read our guide: CMYK vs RGB

How fast can I get my order?+
It depends on the product and quantity. We confirm a delivery date together with your proof, before anything goes to press. Need it urgently? Tell us — we will say honestly what is possible.
What file should I send?+
A print-ready PDF in CMYK at 300 DPI with 3 mm bleed is ideal. Don't worry if you don't have one — we check and fix files for free.
Is there a minimum quantity?+
It depends on the product: visiting cards start at 100, personalised gifts at 1 piece. Bulk orders get better per-unit pricing.
Can I see a sample before a bulk order?+
Ask us when you enquire — for many products we can share a sample or a physical proof before the full run.
