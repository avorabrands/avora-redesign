    Diwali FAQ

    Good to know.

    Diwali is close — the sooner you message, the more options we can hold for you.

    Ask on WhatsApp
    Is there a minimum order?+
    Personalised gifts start from a single piece. Branded hampers work best from 10 units; bigger orders get better per-unit pricing.
    Can you deliver before Diwali?+
    Tell us your date when you message — we confirm a delivery date in writing before you pay. If it's too tight, we'll say so honestly and suggest ready-stock options.
    Where do you deliver?+
    We deliver every order to the one address you share with us, your office, warehouse or home, and send you tracking so you know exactly when it arrives.
    Will I see the hamper before it ships?+
    Always. You approve a digital mock-up of the branding, and we share photos of the final packed hamper.
    Can I mix items from different hampers?+
    Of course — use the hamper builder or tell us your budget per hamper and we'll curate.
